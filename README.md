# Fandaws Sentinel

> *Identity integrity, narrative coherence, and auditability for Fandaws.*

---

## Overview

Fandaws Sentinel is a governance-focused identity integrity service built on top of the Fandaws event substrate.

It transforms raw commitments, actions, and attestations into a structured, auditable narrative identity — while preventing silent erasure, selective omission, and catastrophic misalignment.

Sentinel does not redefine reality.  
It preserves it, interprets it transparently, and subjects it to adversarial review.

---

## Why Sentinel Exists

Modern systems store data.

They do not preserve identity.

Without structural safeguards, narrative systems drift toward:

- Selective omission  
- Biased significance elevation  
- Policy manipulation  
- Coherent falsehood  
- Silent historical revision  

Sentinel is designed to make identity:

- **Tamper-evident**
- **Auditable**
- **Versioned**
- **Governance-aware**
- **Externally challengeable**

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Separation of Services

No single component can:

- Define reality  
- Interpret reality  
- Certify its own interpretation  

Sentinel enforces architectural segregation between:

- Event Ground Truth  
- Significance Classification  
- Narrative Construction  
- Adversarial Audit  
- Governance Policy  

---

### 2. No Silent Erasure

Events cannot be deleted without trace.

Compaction is auditable.  
Decay is policy-bound.  
Revisions are versioned.

---

### 3. Provenance First

All classifications include:

- Confidence normalization  
- Model/version references  
- Policy signatures  
- Replay metadata  

Every narrative claim must cite source events.

---

### 4. Adversarial Audit by Design

Sentinel includes mechanisms to:

- Detect contradiction clusters  
- Surface omission patterns  
- Identify significance skew  
- Escalate authenticity findings  

Healthy identity systems exhibit friction.

Perfect coherence is a red flag.

---

## Architecture

Fandaws Sentinel operates as a layered integrity system:

- **Event Ground Truth Service (EGTS)**  
  Immutable raw events and commitments.

- **Significance & Classification Service (SCS)**  
  Heuristic and model-based significance assignment with confidence normalization.

- **Narrative Construction Service (NCS)**  
  Builds structured identity arcs with citation requirements.

- **Authenticity & Adversarial Audit Service (AAAS)**  
  Independently challenges the narrative.

- **Governance & Policy Service (GPS)**  
  Signs thresholds, decay policies, and calibration rules.

- **Epistemic Health Monitor (EHM)**  
  Tracks systemic drift, disagreement rates, and policy volatility.

---

## What Sentinel Is Not

Sentinel is not:

- A logging system  
- A content moderation engine  
- A moral authority  
- A predictive behavioral model  
- A memory manipulation tool  

It does not decide truth.

It enforces traceability.

## Use Cases

- AI governance and oversight  
- Digital personhood continuity  
- High-integrity audit trails  
- Institutional narrative integrity  
- Commitment tracking and coherence validation  

---

## Threat Model

Sentinel is designed to mitigate:

- Narrative manipulation  
- Selective decay  
- Classification bias drift  
- Policy tampering  
- Coherent falsehood stabilization  
- Silent threshold changes  

It assumes that misalignment is more likely to emerge from subtle bias and structural omission than from overt attack.

---

## Design Goal

The objective is not perfection.

The objective is **irreversibility of exposure**.

If something is wrong, biased, or deceptive,  
the system must make it:

- Discoverable  
- Traceable  
- Attributable  
- Correctable
